MODPLAY(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    modplay - play tracker modules: MOD, MED, S3M, XM

SYNOPSIS
    modplay [-v VOL] [-l] [-s] [-q] [-z] [-r RATE] FILE|FOLDER...
    modplay -K
    modplay -B SECONDS [-r RATE] FILE

DESCRIPTION
    Plays tracker modules through the sound server sndd(8):
      ProTracker, NoiseTracker and Soundtracker MODs ("M.K.", "M!K!",
      "4CHN", "FLT4", the old 15-sample kind), FastTracker's MODs of
      6 to 32 channels ("6CHN", "8CHN", "xxCH", "OCTA", "CD81"),
      MED and OctaMED (MMD0, MMD1, MMD2, MMD3), Scream Tracker 3
      (.s3m) and FastTracker 2 (.xm).
    The kind is found from the file's contents, not its name. Each
    FILE is played in turn; a FOLDER adds its files in name order (255
    songs at most). A file that is not a module is said and passed
    over.
    A module Paula can play as it is (four channels or fewer: MODs and
    MED songs of up to 4 tracks) goes to Paula's four channels, its
    samples in chip RAM, exactly as the tracker played it. Others are
    mixed by the CPU into one 8-bit stereo stream on channels 0 and 1
    (as is a 4-channel one whose samples are over 1 MB), at 16,000 Hz
    up to 8 channels and 11,025 Hz above unless -r says otherwise;
    the other pair stays free.
    On a terminal modplay has a screen of its own: the song's name,
    kind and channels, "Paula" or "mixed"; the position, pattern, row,
    speed, BPM, volume and time; a meter for each channel; and one of
    four views: the pattern being heard, the instruments, the list,
    the keys.

OPTIONS
    -v VOL    the volume, 0-64 (64)
    -l        repeat the song (F00 or FFE still ends it)
    -z        shuffle the list
    -r RATE   the mixing rate in Hz, 4000-28000 (16000 up to 8
              channels, 11025 above): lower for a busy 68020, higher
              on a 68030 or 68060
    -s        no screen: one status line updated in place (the
              position, pattern, row, speed, BPM, the first 8
              channels' meters); the keys still work
    -q        nothing on the terminal and no keys, for scripts
    -K        print the default keys (the keys file's format)
    -B SECS   measure: mix SECS seconds of FILE without playing them
              and say how long it took (the replayer and the mixer)

KEYS
    q, Esc           quit (Ctrl-C always quits)
    space            pause, and go on
    n, p             the next, the previous song
    . , Right Left   the next, the previous pattern (position)
    + = -            louder, softer
    r                repeat: off, the list, the song
    s                shuffle the list (from the next song)
    Tab 1 2 3 ? F1   the next view; pattern, instruments, list, keys
    Help             the keys (as ? and F1)
    Up Down k j      move in the list; Return plays the one chosen
    g                the list: back to the song playing
    [ ]              the channels shown in the pattern view
    Every key can be changed in ~/.config/modplay/keys, written with
    the defaults when it is missing: one "KEY ACTION" a line, <Up>
    <Down> <Left> <Right> <Return> <Esc> <Tab> <Space> <F1>.. for
    special keys, "KEY none" to take one away (the format of files(1)
    and show(1)). A mistake in it is said when modplay ends.

EXIT STATUS
    0 everything played; 1 a file it could not play (it goes on with the
    next); 2 no sound (no server, the channels in use, not enough chip
    RAM). Help is on ?, F1 and Help.

NOTES
    MOD: every ProTracker effect but E0, E8 and EF; FLT8 and tags not
    known are refused.
    MED: sample instruments (8- and 16-bit); synthetic, hybrid and
    multi-octave instruments are silent, MIDI is ignored, as are hold
    and decay (08) and the mix mode's commands but 2E. On Paula a
    note above B-3 plays an octave lower (Paula cannot go faster).
    S3M: Adlib instruments are silent. XM: version 1.04 files.
    Impulse Tracker (.it) is not played. Samples are played as 8-bit
    (16-bit ones by their high byte); the mixer does not interpolate.
    Each tick sent to Paula is applied within about 5 ms of its time;
    a mixed song's keys and screen are about 0.15 s ahead of the sound
    heard. A module's file is read into memory whole (8 MB at most);
    Paula's samples are copied to chip RAM (1 MB at most). The screen
    follows a terminal window's size (drawn anew when it changes).
