HANGMAN(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    hangman - guess the word before the figure is complete

SYNOPSIS
    hangman [-d WORDLIST] [-m MINLEN]

DESCRIPTION
    Guess a hidden word a letter at a time; each wrong letter adds a part
    to the gallows. The words come from /usr/share/dict/words (300 words
    of the wired city: terminals, reactors, signals, rust) or the list
    given with -d, at least MINLEN (6) letters long. Ctrl-D ends, Ctrl-L
    draws the screen again.

FILES
    /usr/share/dict/words   the words, one a line, lower case

EXIT STATUS
    0.

NOTES
    One of the BSD games (NetBSD 10.2's, pinned unchanged in
    ports/bsdgames; the BSD licence: /usr/share/licenses/bsdgames), in
    the games package (zpkg -S games).
    Drawn with Zenix's own small curses (ports/bsdgames/zenix): the
    console, a term window and a telnet line alike.
