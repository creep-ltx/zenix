WUMP(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    wump - hunt the Wumpus in its cave

SYNOPSIS
    wump [-h] [-a ARROWS] [-b BATS] [-p PITS] [-r ROOMS] [-t TUNNELS]

DESCRIPTION
    A dark cave of rooms joined by tunnels; somewhere in it the Wumpus.
    You smell it when it is near, feel the draught of a pit, hear the
    rustle of the bats that carry you off. Move (m) or shoot (s) a
    crooked arrow through a list of rooms; wake the Wumpus and it may
    move, or eat you. "Instructions?" at the start shows the whole story
    through less(1). -h is the hard cave; the other options set how many
    of each there are.

FILES
    /usr/share/games/wump.info   the instructions

EXIT STATUS
    0.

NOTES
    One of the BSD games (NetBSD 10.2's, pinned unchanged in
    ports/bsdgames; the BSD licence: /usr/share/licenses/bsdgames), in
    the games package (zpkg -S games).
    One Zenix patch (ports/bsdgames/zenix/wump.patch): the instructions
    are shown with the shell's "less < wump.info" (no fork on Zenix),
    $PAGER or less.
