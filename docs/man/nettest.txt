NETTEST(1)		Zenix manual

NAME
    nettest - test the network over the loopback

SYNOPSIS
    nettest [-p ADDR] [-n BYTES] CHECK...
    nettest serve tcp|udp PORT echo|sink
    nettest serve tcp PORT flood

DESCRIPTION
    A test program for the network server, netd(8), and libz's BSD
    socket calls (DESIGN D19 step 4). Each check starts the servers it
    needs (nettest itself, as "nettest serve ...") on 127.0.0.1 and
    prints "nettest CHECK: PASS (...)" or "nettest CHECK: FAIL (...)"
    with what it saw:

    tcp       100 KB echoed in 1,000-byte pieces, the same bytes back;
              getpeername and getsockname; 65,000 bytes in one blocking
              send to a server that counts and sums them, shutdown, the
              count and sum back, then the end of the stream
    udp       datagrams of 1 to 1,400 bytes echoed whole, from the
              server's address; a datagram longer than the buffer is
              cut and the rest dropped
    poll      poll waits its time when there is nothing to read, says
              writable at once, wakes on the echo; FIONREAD; select
    nonblock  fcntl F_GETFL and F_SETFL O_NONBLOCK; accept and recv
              then say "try again"; a non-blocking connect, POLLOUT and
              SO_ERROR; accept4 with SOCK_NONBLOCK; on a terminal
              F_SETFL O_NONBLOCK is refused (not yet)
    names     inet_pton, inet_ntop, inet_aton, inet_addr, inet_ntoa;
              getaddrinfo with a number and a service name, "localhost"
              from /etc/hosts, an unknown name, AI_NUMERICHOST
    many      connections until netd's limit (12 at most; the small
              profile allows 4 over the loopback, each takes two TCP
              ends): the next is refused at once ("no buffer space"),
              and every one made works
    perm      root may bind port 80, a user may not, nor change the
              interfaces or the routes; SOCK_RAW is refused
    restart   SA_RESTART against netd: a recv and an accept that an
              alarm's handler interrupts go on; without SA_RESTART, or
              with SO_RCVTIMEO set, recv says "interrupted"; a send that
              waited from its start goes on and sends all, in order
    partial   a 30,000-byte send to a reader that waits 2 s, interrupted
              by an alarm after part of it went: the count so far comes
              back (no restart), the rest is sent after, and the reader
              gets every byte once, in order
    sigpipe   the peer closed: recv says end of file; sends with
              MSG_NOSIGNAL say "connection reset", then "broken pipe";
              without it the sender ends by SIGPIPE (141); with SIGPIPE
              ignored it gets "broken pipe"
    down      netd killed while a child waits in recv: the child gets
              ENETDOWN at once; the next socket starts a new netd that
              serves a new connection
    drvperm   the drivers' system call (SYS_drv) is refused to a program
              that is not netd's child, root included, and a raw
              message to net shaped as a driver's transfer is refused
    giveup    netd killed until the kernel gives it up (five ends in a
              minute): then a socket says "network is down" at once
              (root's ifup -a brings it back: tools/netd-test.sh)
    asm       netd's checksum and copy in assembler against C, for
              every alignment and many lengths, and their speed in this
              machine's E-clock counts (an emulator's counts are not a
              real Amiga's speed)
    hang      prints "hang: waiting" and waits in accept (for ^C)
    listen    a listener on port 7010 with one connection to it and a
              UDP socket on 127.0.0.1:7011, kept for 30 seconds (for
              netstat)
    slip      through SLIP to the PC's test servers (tools/slip-test.sh,
              on 10.0.2.2, or -p ADDR: a PC on the LAN running
              tools/slip-host.py with SLIP_HOST_ADDR, as the A1200
              checklist for the CM4's Ethernet does): the checks below
    sliptcp   10 KB echoed by TCP port 7100
    slipget   100 KB from port 7102, checked byte by byte, and the
              bytes a second (-n BYTES: another amount)
    slipudp   datagrams echoed by UDP port 7101
    slipdns   amiga.zenix.test looked up through 10.0.2.3
    slipflood a SYN flood: four listeners (ports 7200-7203) that never
              accept, while the PC opens 400 connections to them; 100
              sleeps of 20 ms timed meanwhile, /bin/true started; netd
              must not end, its memory stays in its profile, and a
              connection to the PC works afterwards

EXIT STATUS
    The number of checks that failed.

    nettest serve tcp PORT flood plays a hostile telnet board for
    tools/bbs-test.sh: it sends IAC DO 200 over and over to its first
    caller and never reads, until the caller is gone or 120 s pass.

NOTES
    A test program, run by tools/netd-test.sh and tools/slip-test.sh in
    the emulator. The ports 7001-7014 must be free.

SEE ALSO
