GRIDRUN(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    gridrun - a light-cycle duel on a dead city's power grid

SYNOPSIS
    gridrun

DESCRIPTION
    A game for Zenix Desktop, written in Lua with the zenix module. You
    ride the cyan trail, a daemon of the grid rides the magenta one; a
    cycle that hits the wall or any trail is derezzed, and the other wins
    the round. The score is in the window's title.

    Keys: the arrows turn, space starts the next round, p pauses, q or
    Escape ends (so does the window's close gadget).

    Its pictures, its sound effects and its music (a four-channel module
    played through sndd) are made by the script itself when it starts:
    no files besides it. The colours are the nearest the desktop's theme
    has. It needs the desktop (zdesk) and the sound server; without sound
    it plays silent.

ENVIRONMENT
    GRIDRUN_SEED    the daemon's random numbers (the time by default)
    GRIDRUN_LOG     set: a line on standard output for each round, key and
                    the music (for tests)

FILES
    /bin/gridrun    the game itself, a Lua script started by its #! line:
                    read it, change it, run your copy

NOTES
    In the games package (zpkg -S games), which brings the lua package.
    Ours, GPL 2. On a 14 MHz 68020 it takes a few seconds to make its
    sounds and music before the window shows the grid.

SEE ALSO
    zenix-lua(1), lua(1), worm(1)
