UNLHA(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    unlha, unlzx - list, test and unpack LhA and LZX archives

SYNOPSIS
    unlha l|t|x [-f] [-q] [-d folder] [-L megabytes] archive [name...]
    unlzx l|t|x [-f] [-q] [-d folder] [-L megabytes] archive [name...]

DESCRIPTION
    unlha reads LhA archives (.lha, .lzh), unlzx LZX archives (.lzx): the
    same program under two names. They unpack only; neither makes archives.
    The first argument says what to do; a leading "-" is allowed.

    l    list: size, packed size, date, protection bits, method, name, and
         a filenote on a line of its own
    t    test: unpack to nothing, every checksum checked
    x    unpack into the current folder or -d folder, making folders

    Names after the archive pick members: a member by its whole name, or a
    folder with all under it. A name not found says so, with exit status 1.

    LhA: methods -lh0-, -lz4-, -lh1-, -lh4- to -lh7-, -lhd-, header levels
    0, 1 and 2. LZX: stored and normal, merged groups too. Another method is
    listed, and answers "not supported" with exit status 1.

OPTIONS
    -f     overwrite. Without it a file already there is left alone and
           said. With it the old name is removed first, so a soft link in
           its place is replaced, never written through.
    -q     quiet: no line per member, errors only
    -d D   unpack into folder D, which must exist
    -L N   the limit on the bytes unpacked, in MB (default 512, at most 4000)

EXAMPLES
    unlha l game.lha
    unlha t game.lha
    mkdir game; unlha x -d game game.lha
    unlzx x -q demo.lzx docs/readme.txt

NOTES
    An archive is not trusted. A member is refused, with a message and exit
    status 1, if its name starts with "/", has a ".." part, is a link, or
    does not fit 255 bytes; the others are still unpacked. No link is ever
    made, and a soft link on the way to a target is not followed.

    Before unpacking, the sizes are added up; over the limit nothing is
    done. In an LZX group a member costs all the group's bytes before it,
    even when only that member is asked for.

    A member that fails its checksum, or is damaged, does not stay on the
    disk. A damaged group's later members answer the same error.

    Valid UTF-8 names stay; anything else is read as Latin-1, the Amiga's,
    and becomes UTF-8. Control characters become "_".

    Dates are kept, read as UTC; a folder's date is not. Modes: LhA's Unix
    mode if it has one, without set-user-id; else from the protection bits:
    r gives r--r--r--, w -w-------, e --x--x--x (nothing at all: rw-------).
    h, s, p, a and filenotes have no place on Zenix: listed, not stored.
    A filenote is shown up to 79 bytes.

    LZX 1.21 without its year 2000 patch wrote wrong years after 1999, as
    far back as 1990: such a date is shown as stored.

    Memory: the largest window is 64 KB (-lh7- and LZX), about 73 KB in
    all, enough on a 2 MB A1200. Archives of 2 GB or more cannot be read.
    LZX block type 1 has never been seen, and answers "not supported".

EXIT STATUS
    0 all well; 1 a member failed, was refused or was not found; 2 usage, no
    such archive, a damaged archive (members before the damage are still
    unpacked), or over the limit.
