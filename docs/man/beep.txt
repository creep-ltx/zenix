BEEP(1)				Zenix manual

NAME
    beep - ring the terminal bell

SYNOPSIS
    beep

DESCRIPTION
    Writes the bell character (BEL, control-G) to standard output. The
    terminal answers with a short tone from Paula, the Amiga's sound chip
    (880 Hz, 80 ms, both channels, a quarter of full volume). Any program
    that prints BEL does the same.
    The first beep after boot logs one line in the kernel log (see dmesg)
    that says whether the sound hardware reacted; this is the check on a
    machine where the tone cannot be heard.

EXIT STATUS
    Always 0.

NOTES
    Not done: choosing pitch or length, other sounds, a sound server.
    The pitch is calculated for a PAL clock.
