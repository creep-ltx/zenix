CONMIRROR(8)			Zenix manual

NAME
    conmirror - switch the serial copy of the console off or on

SYNOPSIS
    conmirror off|on

DESCRIPTION
    Everything drawn on the console on the screen is also sent to the
    serial port (115200 baud), so a terminal or the test rig on the other
    end of a cable sees it. The port sends about 11.5 KB a second, far
    slower than the screen draws. While something has been received on
    the port in the last 10 seconds, the copy waits for the port, so the
    other end loses nothing - and the screen is held to the port's speed.
    With nothing received since boot or for 10 seconds, what does not fit
    the 4 KB send buffer is dropped and counted (the kernel log says so).

    conmirror off stops the copy: the screen never waits for the port.
    conmirror on starts it again (from the next byte; nothing missed is
    sent). The kernel's own log (boot messages, panics) still goes to the
    port either way. Only root may switch it.

EXIT STATUS
    0 done, 1 refused (not root), 2 a wrong argument.

NOTES
    Not done: asking whether the copy is on, a per-console setting (the
    copy always follows the console on the screen). The setting is lost at
    a restart: the copy starts on. The test rig depends on the copy: a
    script that switches it off sees nothing more on the serial port.
