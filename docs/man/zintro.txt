ZINTRO(1)			Zenix manual

NAME
    zintro - the Zenix intro: a logo, copper bars, stars and a scroller

SYNOPSIS
    zintro [-v] [-q]

DESCRIPTION
    An Amiga demo intro in the old way, on a screen of its own (DESIGN
    D55): the ZENIX logo in bevelled steel with a toxic green rim, copper
    bars sweeping through the background, a starfield of three speeds, a
    turning wireframe shape, and a scroller at the bottom whose letters
    the Copper shades line by line. A four-channel ProTracker module
    plays through the sound server (sndd): A minor, slow and heavy.

    Esc, q or a click ends it and the desktop is back. Left Amiga+N puts
    the desktop in front while it goes on playing; Left Amiga+N again
    brings it back.

OPTIONS
    -v      say the screen, and at the end the frames drawn and missed
    -q      no music

NOTES
    Everything in it (the logo, the font, the text, the music) is made
    from numbers by tools/mkintro.py when Zenix is built: nothing of
    anyone else's. Lores, 4 planes, two pictures in chip RAM (80 KB on a
    PAL machine); the CPU draws, the Copper colours. It needs the desktop
    on the chipset's display: on a graphics card (zenixboot RTG) it says
    so and ends. Without the sound server, or with its channels busy
    (modplay playing), it runs silent and says why. On NTSC the screen is
    200 lines and the layout follows.

    In the games package (zpkg -S games). Ours, GPL 2.
