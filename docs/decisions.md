**ZINE's five questions answered (owner 2026-10-30, "I agree on the five questions"; DESIGN D29 section 15):** (1) root and a `zine` group may hand the machine over, only at the Amiga's own console or through Wasabi, never from ssh, telnet or serial; (2) all of Zenix is paused while the program runs; (3) files the program writes are gone afterwards, Zenix's disks untouched; (4) on the 1260 ZINE may use the owner's own `68060.library` copied into `/etc/zine/libs/` (never shipped); (5) during a program Ctrl-Amiga-Amiga means "back to Zenix".
**ZINE runs the program under Zenix, no restarts (owner 2026-10-30: "This demo runs under Zenix. Zenix just pointing the demo to the hardware that it needs"):** D29 revision 2's design (Zenix sleeps, the machine restarts into the ROM's AmigaOS and back) was not what the owner wanted and is replaced: Zenix stays in charge, loads the AmigaOS executable itself, gives it a small AmigaOS of Zenix's own (the calls demos use: memory, taking over, graphics/blitter/copper, files) and the hardware it asks for, and when it ends puts its own screen, keyboard and interrupts back and carries on at the prompt. The five answers above stay where they still apply; D29 is rewritten (revision 3).
**ZINE revision 3's questions answered as recommended (owner 2026-10-30, "your proposals are fine"):** (1) no starting under ZINE through `wasabi run`: only at the console (typed there or through Wasabi's keys); (2) Ctrl-Amiga-Amiga during a program is a clean restart into Zenix (disks saved first, open programs lost), on every machine: the reset-capture hook ("path B") is dropped (a safety check stops building reset-surviving capture code, as boot viruses use; 2026-10-30); `zine` says so before it hands over; (3) others logged in (ssh, telnet) are warned and paused; (4) the free NDK 3.2's interface files (includes, .fd) may be used for names and numbers only, never code.
