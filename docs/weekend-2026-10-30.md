# The owner's weekend, from Saturday 2026-10-31: one checklist

Everything for the weekend in order, with links to the detailed checklists instead of copies of them. The session on
the PC builds the package on Saturday morning, from the newest main, with `RELEASE=1` and the release key (`tools/mkcardpkg.sh`, into `build/cardpkg/`), and is there to help;
tell it which step you are on. **Photo** means a photo of the screen, **note** means write the line or number down.
Where a command has a `|`: in Zenix that is Shift and the key left of Backspace; `~` is Shift and the key left of 1.

Machines: the **PiStorm** A1200 (the card), the **stock** A1200 (PiStorm out, the DoM on the IDE port, 2 MB chip RAM
only), the **060** (the Apollo 1260 in, the same DoM).

## (a) Update the card and start the new Zenix (PiStorm, about 30 minutes)

Follow [card-package.md](card-package.md) (the package's `README.md`, the same text): step 1 (the boot partition, from
AmigaOS), step 2 (the root, card in the PC), step 3 (the first start). What to look for at the first start: zenixboot
names `Zenix/zenix`, `uname` the package's version, `mount` shows `/home`, your users are there. Step 4 (an update the
new way, and back) is optional and takes 10 minutes; it is the first real test of the self-updates (DESIGN D15).

## (b) The serial cable: cu, then the network over the cable (PiStorm first)

The cable: the USB RS-232 adapter on the PC, the DB25 null-modem end on the A1200's serial port.

1. **On the PC:** plug the adapter in; `ls /dev/ttyUSB*` shows its name (say `/dev/ttyUSB0`). Once: your user must be
   in the group `uucp` ([network-serial.md](network-serial.md), "On the PC").
2. **cu** (man page [cu](man/cu.txt)): on the PC start a login on the adapter:
   `sudo systemctl start serial-getty@ttyUSB0`. In Zenix as root: `cu`, then Return: the PC's login prompt must
   come. Log in, try `ls`, `uname -a`, a Swedish letter, Ctrl-C on `sleep 100`. End with Return, `~`, `.`.
   **Note** whether the prompt came, whether the text looked right, and anything garbled (**photo**).
3. Then on the PC: `sudo systemctl stop serial-getty@ttyUSB0`.
4. **SLIP** ([network-serial.md](network-serial.md), the whole page): on the PC (once)
   `make -C tools/host/zenix-slirp`, then `build/zenix-slirp /dev/ttyUSB0` (it keeps running). In Zenix as root:
   ```
   ifup sl0
   ifconfig sl0
   ping -c 3 10.0.2.2
   ```
   `/etc/network/interfaces` on the card and on the DoM already has the `sl0` lines. Expected: `sl0` UP and RUNNING
   with 10.0.2.15, three answers from 10.0.2.2. **Note** the `ping` times. Keep it up for (d); afterwards
   `ifdown sl0` gives the port back (and on the PiStorm the Ethernet is the way out again).

## (d) ssh to the PC, telnet to a BBS

- **ssh** over the Ethernet: [a1200-checklist-network.md](a1200-checklist-network.md) step 11. Over the cable (SLIP
  up, as in (b)), the PC is `10.0.2.2` and no firewall rule is needed: `ssh you@10.0.2.2`. **Note** the seconds from
  Return to the PC's prompt each way, and `free` on Amiga+F2 while the session is up.
- **telnet** to a board: the same checklist, step 8 (`telnet HOST PORT`, `-p` for a PC board). **Note** how a screen
  looks (**photo** one) and how long a 1 MB download takes.

## (e) The measurements (each machine; on the stock A1200 and the 060 they count most)

Always after a cold start (switched off and on), as root on console 1, nothing else running. Write the numbers into the
table at the end.

1. **Free memory with one login:** `free`. **Note** the chip and fast lines.
2. **Free memory with four logins** (stock A1200): log in as `user` on Amiga+F2, F3 and F4 (no password), back to
   Amiga+F1, `free`. **Note** it. (Ten logins, F2-F10, if you have the patience: note that too.)
3. **The console's speed at each number of colours** (the cost of the colour console, DESIGN D14, and the scroll
   test that decides whether the screen buffer can be halved): at the machine's own setting first,
   ```
   bench
   time seq 3000
   ```
   **Photo** `bench`'s screen; **note** the `time` line. Then the same at 2 and at 16 colours, each followed by a
   restart (the setting counts from the next start):
   ```
   echo colours 2 > /etc/console
   reboot zenix
   ```
   (log in again, `bench`, `time seq 3000`, `free`), then `echo colours 16 > /etc/console` and `reboot zenix` (the
   same three), and at the end `echo colours auto > /etc/console` (the setting as shipped).
4. **The crypto timings:** [a1200-checklist-network.md](a1200-checklist-network.md) step 10 (`cryptotest`,
   `cryptotest time 3`, `cryptotest mul`), on the stock A1200 and the 060. **Photo** each screen.
5. **The ssh login time:** from (d), on the PiStorm (Ethernet) and on the stock A1200 (the cable): the seconds from
   Return to the PC's prompt.
6. **The disk check** (DESIGN D17: how long a check of a disk takes on a real machine):
   ```
   mount
   umount /home
   time fsck.ext2 -n ide0pN
   mount -a
   ```
   with `ide0pN` the device `mount` showed for `/home` (on the card `/home` is 3 GB, on the DoM 64 MB). **Note** the
   `time` line and the last line `fsck.ext2` printed. `mount -a` puts `/home` back.
7. **The cost of a symbolic link across a mount** (DESIGN D26: the target is that a link adds less than a tenth to a
   command's start on the stock A1200):
   ```
   ln -s /home /tmp/lh; ln -s /bin/ls /home/lsl
   time sh -c 'for i in $(seq 200); do test -e /tmp/lh; done'
   time sh -c 'for i in $(seq 200); do test -e /home; done'
   time /home/lsl / > /dev/null
   time /bin/ls / > /dev/null
   rm /tmp/lh /home/lsl
   ```
   **Note** the four `real` lines (the first two: 200 lookups through a link and directly; the last two: `ls` started
   through a link on `/home` and directly).

8. **The console's text and box lines** (audit block 3 B3-17 and B3-18: plain text about 4% faster, box-drawing lines
   scrolled without a search; counted on the PC, this is the real time):
   ```
   printf '\342\224\200\342\224\200\342\224\200\342\224\200\342\224\200 box\n' > /tmp/b1
   yes "$(cat /tmp/b1)" | head -n 3000 > /tmp/box
   time cat /tmp/box
   time seq 3000
   rm /tmp/b1 /tmp/box
   ```
   **Note** the two `real` lines (3,000 lines with box characters; 3,000 lines of plain digits).

## (f) The DoM on the stock A1200 and on the 060

1. Write the DoM from the PC: [card-package.md](card-package.md), "The DoM" (find its name with `lsblk` before and
   after connecting it, never guess it).
2. **Stock A1200** (PiStorm out, DoM in): switch on; the menu starts Zenix after 3 seconds. **Photo** the screen if it
   stops anywhere (the screen colours of the start are in [pi-first-boot.md](pi-first-boot.md)). Then (b) steps 2-4
   with the cable (the stock A1200's only serial port: `ifdown sl0` before `cu`), (d) over the cable, (e) 1-6.
3. **060** (the Apollo in, the same DoM): switch on; the same. Then (e) 1, 3, 4 and 6. If the menu or zenixboot does
   not come up on the 060, **photo** and tell the session.

## (g) What to send back

- The photos and the notes, with the machine each is from, and the filled table.
- From each machine once, as root: `dmesg > /home/user/dmesg-MACHINE.txt` after a start, so the session can read it
  from the card or the DoM later (or copy it over ssh: `cat /home/user/dmesg-MACHINE.txt | ssh you@10.0.2.2 'cat >
  dmesg-MACHINE.txt'`). After anything odd, the next start's `dmesg -p` holds the previous start's log: save it the
  same way before the next restart.
- From step 2 of (a): the lines `write-root.sh` printed. Nothing else on the card needs changing by hand.

**Updating from now on, no AmigaOS step (DESIGN D22 amendment 2, approved by you 2026-10-31; once the session has published the release with it):** as root,
1. `zpkg -Syu`: this brings `zpkg-new`. It still says to copy the system from AmigaOS: ignore that.

3. `reboot` (Zenix): the new kernel and base are installed as you start.
4. `zpkg -Syu`: the programs that waited for it.
